Formulas
=TQ.BALANCE(account, fromPeriod, [toPeriod], [subsidiary], [department], [class], [location], [filters])

=TQ.ACCOUNTNAME(account)

=TQ.PERIOD(period, [shift])

=TQ.LIST(kind, [filter], [from], [to], [hideZero], [subsidiary], [show]) spills a list that grows by itself: =TQ.LIST("accounts","6000-6999"), =TQ.LIST("departments",,"FYS","CM",TRUE) (only those with a balance). For formulas next to it, list the numbers only ("number" as the last argument) in A10, then =TQ.ACCOUNTNAME(A10#) and =TQ.BALANCE(A10#,$B$8) grow with it.

Periods can be relative: CM (this month) CM-1 (last month) LY (same month last year) FYS / FYE (first / last month of the year) QS QE FYS-12 (start of last year). Year to date: from FYS to CM. With a month cell: =TQ.PERIOD($B$8,"FYS").

Totals work as the account: type:Income type:Bank,AcctRec calc:NetIncome calc:NetIncomeYTD calc:RetainedEarnings 4000, 4100-4199 calc:Days

Filters: Sales, Bar not Sales Food* (with everything under it) Parent* (subsidiary, consolidated) Brand: Premium; Name: Al Noor

Move to another PC

Take your setup to a new computer: the NetSuite connection (never a password or key), the licence, the logo, your saved filter sets and this workbook's report layouts, in one file.

Export from this PC

Import on this PC

Choose the file exported on the other PC. Nothing changes until you press Import.

The workbook itself also carries its layouts: saving it and opening it on the other PC works too. The export adds the connection, licence, logo and filter sets, and rebuilds the layouts in any workbook.
